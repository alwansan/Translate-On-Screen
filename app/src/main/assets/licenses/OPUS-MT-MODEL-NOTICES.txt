Each language pair downloads encoder_model_quantized.onnx,
decoder_model_merged_quantized.onnx, source.spm and vocab.json.
The application's OfflineModelManifest.kt specifies all file sizes and SHA-256
digests, and downloads verify them before installation. No model data is bundled
in the APK. Installation requires the user's explicit download action.

The source-card revisions above identify inspected license statements. They do
not assert that the ONNX conversion used those exact source revisions. The
converted-model cards identify their corresponding base-model repositories.

The application independently implements the tokenizer in Kotlin. The
SentencePiece and protobuf software runtimes are not included in the app.
The optional models' Apache-2.0 terms, ONNX Runtime's MIT terms and the new
application code's Apache-2.0 terms are separate license grants.
